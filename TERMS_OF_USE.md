## 1. What the app does

VoiceTodo converts spoken sentences into structured to-dos (dates, recurrence, categories, reminders) using AI, and stores your to-dos locally on your device. The conversion ("AI extraction") is an online service: it requires an internet connection and is processed on our servers and by third-party AI providers, as described in our [Privacy Policy](https://qingqingyu.github.io/voicetodo-privacy/).

## 2. AI output disclaimer

To-do structuring is generated automatically by an AI model. It is usually right, but it can be wrong: dates, times, recurrence rules, categories, or priorities may be misparsed — especially for ambiguous input ("this weekend", unusual formats, mixed languages). **Always review extracted to-dos before confirming them, and verify anything important (deadlines, payments, medication).** We are not liable for consequences of relying on unreviewed AI output.

## 3. Free tier and Pro subscription

**Free tier.** The app is free to download and includes a limited number of AI extractions per day (3 per day). Local features (browsing, editing, completing to-dos, widgets) are not metered.

**VoiceTodo Pro.** Pro is an auto-renewable subscription that increases the daily AI extraction allowance to a high fair-use cap and removes ads (the app has no ads to begin with).

- Pricing: US$4.99/month or US$39.99/year (local prices may vary per App Store region)
- 7-day free trial where offered; if you cancel before the trial ends, you are not charged
- Payment is charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase
- The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period
- You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime in your App Store account settings (Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions)

**Fair use.** Pro's daily allowance is a fixed cap chosen to cover heavy personal use while keeping the service sustainable — it is high, but it is not literally unlimited, and the current limit is shown in the app. We may adjust the cap with app updates; if we lower it materially, the change applies to future billing periods, and you may cancel before renewal.

**Service costs.** Each AI extraction incurs real per-request costs. The tier limits exist to keep the service available and honest; circumventing them (multiple accounts, automation, or other abuse) may result in suspension of the service for the affected device.
